Tips for a stronger password
  • Use a passphrase of 4–5 random words instead of a single word.
  • Aim for 12+ characters — length matters more than complexity.
  • Never reuse the same password across different sites.
  • Avoid personal info: names, birthdays, pet names.
  • Use a password manager to generate and store unique passwords.
